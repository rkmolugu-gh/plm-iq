Help center

Everything you need to find your way around PLM-IQ while the platform is in early access.

Getting started

  • Open your workspace at tenant.edition.localhost.com
  • Sign in with any credentials during early access
  • The dashboard is your landing page after sign-in

Using the graph explorer

  • Browse vertices, edges, and edge annotations per tab
  • Press GView on a vertex to traverse its neighborhood
  • Filter by source, relation, or target; click nodes to walk the graph
  • The tree below each diagram mirrors the filtered result

Lifecycle quick reference

  • draft being authored
  • in review awaiting approval
  • approved approved, not yet live
  • released live and immutable

Need more help?
