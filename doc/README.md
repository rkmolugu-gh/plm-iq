PLM-IQ
AI-Native Product Lifecycle Intelligence**

PLM-IQ is an AI-native platform that reimagines Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) for the era of intelligent software. It unifies engineering knowledge, CAD assets, technical documents, and product data into a living, searchable knowledge fabric that continuously learns and evolves.

Rather than treating AI as a feature, PLM-IQ is designed around autonomous reasoning, semantic understanding, and agentic workflows, enabling engineering teams to collaborate with knowledge instead of merely managing files.

Key Concepts
🤖 AI-Native Architecture
🧠 Agentic Engineering Workflows
🔍 Hybrid Retrieval (Keyword + Semantic + Graph)
📚 Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
🎯 Multimodal Understanding
🌐 Knowledge Graph Intelligence
🌳 Git-Native Versioning
☁️ Cloud-Native SaaS
🔌 API-First & Event-Driven
🏢 Multi-Tenant Enterprise Platform
Vision
PLM-IQ transforms engineering repositories into an intelligent reasoning system where products, documents, CAD models, requirements, software, and manufacturing knowledge become interconnected, explainable, and discoverable through natural language.

Engineers interact with products through conversations, AI agents, and contextual search rather than navigating disconnected folders, databases, and legacy interfaces.

Domains
